# GDWJ #96

The theme for the Godot Wild Jam #96 is '***Charge***'
The wildcards are:
**Modularity** - Parts can be combined to create new items
**Best if used by** - In-game resources lose value over time
**Cutie** - Add something cute

After a bit of thinking and staring at a wall I think I want to dabble with magnetic *charge*.
My idea is a simple parkouring game where you go through a series of levels with magnetic props in them which you can use your handheld magnet to bounce off.
All of your magnets have a 'charge' that starts at 100% at the start of every level. Holding a magnet out drains its charge slowly, and you can press a key to make it waaayyyyy stronger for a second, but drain a lot of charge.
You can get different handheld magnets which do different things too.

I'm writing this as I'm doing it, so it may change, but here's how magnets work in the game (definitely not realistic):
- We fetch every magnet on the current map every frame (yeah FUCK YOU people with the bad computers, I can't be bothered not to)
- If the distance between us and the magnet is above the range we skip the rest of the loop (\**pat pat*\* yes you're welcome my bad computer fellows :3)
- We calculate the strength by multiplying `strength * currentDist` where `currentDist` is $ \frac{distance}{effectiveRange} $ (wow latex is cool, never done that before)
- The player's magnet is negatively charged, meaning we negate the force if the magnet we're looping over isn't positive. (I've put a bracket note on every other line so I'm legally required to put one here too - my boss is mean)
- Finally, we add the force `strength * direction * delta` to our velocity, and we're done :3
