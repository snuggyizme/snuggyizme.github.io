The photo viewer part of PreEdit (epic name yeah?) has zooming and panning so you can check details of the photo ( e.g. is it focused ( FUCKFUCKFUCKFUCKFUCK ITS NOT FOCUSED >:C ) ) to deduce if it's worth keeping. You can press `SPACE` to recentre the photo in the viewport (although I couldn't be fucked fixing that for when the photo is sideways. Oh by the way you can uh- wait I'll just let un-indented me explain it`*1`) and you can press `C` to alternate between 0° and -90° rotation for vertical photos.

Now you know what you feel about your photo, you need to actually do anything with it. I have five pills for you, good friend:
- (Q) Keep - nothing happens to the photo. It stays in the slideshow when you press Export and Reduce so you can come back and choose what to do with it.
- (A) Delete Src - the photo is deleted from the session memory and also permanently deleted from where it originated.
- (S) Delete - the photo is only deleted from the session memory.
- (D) Export - the photo is deleted from the session memory and a copy of it is exported.
- (F) Export & Delete Src - the photo is deleted from the memory session and also permanently deleted from where it originated. A copy of it is exported.

When you press 'Export and Reduce', the app looks through the slideshow of images and what you picked as their deposition (the options above) and does its business with them, leaving you with only the photos set to 'Keep' left in the slideshow. This sounds lame but it's kind of useful- I could not be able to decide on which photo of the same bird I like more, so I leave it on the default setting of keep and move on. Then I come back when I export to ~20 photos (taken from a set of 411 photos) and make my decision then. (In the end I just deleted both bird pictures lol. **FUCK THAT BIRD IN PARTICULAR!!!**)

### 2. What's in this photo?
You can use the 'group' field on every photo to decide how your album is categorized.
When a photo's group is left blank and it is exported, it'll end up in `PICTURES/PreEdit/export/--projectName--/`. That's great and all, but what if I have 411 photos and I want to group up all the photos of Joe and all the photos of the creek and all the photos of birds in their own seperate folders? When a photo is in a group, it's exported to `PICTURES/PreEdit/export/--projectName--/--groupName--/`

That's kinda the whole group system. I mean theres some keybinds, `R` and then `ENTER` opens the group box. Because idfk how to make it happen without `ENTER`. So deal with it.

`W` and `E` cycle through all the groups mentioned elsewhere in the slideshow, so you don't have to type it in manually every time.
