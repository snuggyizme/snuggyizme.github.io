# Sky after Rain

I've started heaps of small projects recently and it's not very good.\
But with how near to completion my main project is (  sad, it never got a single real ramble :c  ), I do want a replacement 'low effort' game where I can just add whatever whenever. My idea for this of course was to make the same game but better. That evolved into a weird mix of my main game, Smash-like fighting games and Terraria, which I call... fuck I haven't got a name for this one either lmao.

![IMG - A hot sword. Very attractive](/assets/imgs/7.png)

It's a fighting game like smash bros, with defined classes with abilities and a super each. It does, however, have a lot of defining features:
- Mouse aiming instead of directional keys (directional keys are also not as important for differentiating between moves too, ~~because I hate animation~~ \**cough cough*\* because I don't really love the control scheme as it limits your motion or your attacking, depending on which one you're least focused on)
- Guns. Fighting games seem to be scared of guns.
- A unique scoring system. There is no 'deathmatch' or tracker of who got the most points. You simply have a killstreak and must maintain the highest one. I'm not sure if I want there to be any win condition at all, maybe there is a goal for the killstreak or maybe it's simply a fun session to goof off and see who gets the best.
- Super Battery is a mechanic idea I had that has 1000% been used already but it's quite simply backup reserves of your super. Supers are charged by getting hits, and I'm considering if it should be 1pt per hit or a fraction of the damage. Each character has an independantly balanced amount of points required to get a super and, super battery cooldown (to stop you spamming 9 supers at once) and also the super battery's capacity.

Unfortunately the mouse aiming, which i do really want, means this has to either have controller support or be online multiplayer, and since I like having online multiplayer I just made it. Uh so y'know how I said **"I do want a replacement 'low effort' game where I can just add whatever whenever."**? Yeah that's not going well, the game isn't that easy to work on.\
BUT. I am getting better at netcode and I know what to do most of the time. I decided against using GodotSteam again after my first attempt at online MP as it makes testing multiplayer bugs / features literally impossible and although I could do a bit of singleplayer testing, the upsides were just not worth it at all. If I really want to add it I'll add it at the last minute.
