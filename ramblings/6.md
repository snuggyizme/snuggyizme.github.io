# An ending and a revival. Baguette.

I've decided to stop working on the jam game. It wasn't working well enough into the idea and it was putting a lot of stress onto me and my schedule. I'm pretty sad I didn't get it done in time and its kinda sad to look at the itch jam page timer still up and moving but I can't keep working on it. I did take away a lot from it, mainly a lot of developments on my visual style but also I've realised I need to research how to implement my ideas before I settle on that game in a jam, as I didn't realise how brutally impossible online leaderboards would be to get up for free, for a jam game, in 2 days. As soon as I researched it I didn't even want to try.

But enough sad yapping, what am I doing now?\
