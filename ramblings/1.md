## Movement
There are two types of walljumps in .... the game .... :
- Wall**bounces**, where you jump while on a wall and are pushed away from it and up a bit.
- Wall**jumps** are executed the same as wallbounces, but while walking into the wall as you jump. They throw you higher up and not as far out from the wall, and you can only do three before touching the ground again.

You can also double tap a directional key to perform a dash.

Me doing some walljumps, wallbounces and then dashes:
![GIF - Me doing some walljumps, wallbounces, and then dashes](/assets/gifs/3.gif)

There are some quirks with dashes that I find quite interesting. Firstly, theres no cooldown on them (besides your framerate), so the best way to play is to spam the directional keys and hold jump to b-hop, which looks kind of funny and scares everyone who tries my game in person :P
Secondly, due to how the dash adds speed based off your current velocity, not your current *horizontal* velocity,  it's entirely possible to dash at just the right time *just as you jump* to get a bunch more vertical speed, which I call weird dashing. Because it's weird. I first noticed it happening when I jump around on the endgame map (see the first gif again) but I still can't get the timing right myself.
