## Movement
There are two types of walljumps in .... the game .... :
- Wall**bounces**, where you jump while on a wall and are pushed away from it and up a bit.
- Wall**jumps** are executed the same as wallbounces, but while walking into the wall as you jump. They throw you higher up and not as far out from the wall, and you can only do three before touching the ground again.

You can also double tap a directional key to perform a dash.
